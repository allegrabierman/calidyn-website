You know the answer. It doesn’t always land.

A senior stakeholder pushes back and the recommendation starts to soften. A strong idea gets buried under too much detail. A meeting in your second language moves faster than you’d like.

None of this says anything about how capable you are, but it shapes how capable you look. Calidyn works on that gap, especially for people who work across cultures or use English as a working language.

Start with evidence. Then work on the real thing.

First, the Communication Diagnostic

Every engagement starts here. We look at how you communicate across real work situations, like presenting a recommendation, handling a challenge or writing to someone senior, and see where your point starts to get lost. You get a clear written report on what’s working and what’s worth developing.

Then, the Executive Sprint

From there, we work on your actual emails, meetings and presentations through rewriting, rehearsal and feedback, so the improvement shows up in the work you’re already doing.

The goal is range, not a new personality. Nobody needs to sound more native to be taken seriously.

For people whose expertise is not in question.

Managers, specialists and leaders in international companies whose communication doesn’t always carry what they know, especially in a second language, under pressure or in front of senior leadership.

What happens next

  1. Request. Tell me about your role and where communication feels harder than it should. I reply personally within two working days.
  2. The Diagnostic. About 30-35 minutes online, in your own time: short recorded responses to real work scenarios, plus one writing sample.
  3. The Live Check. A 15-minute call, based on your responses. We take one of your real situations and work through it together.
  4. Your feedback session. We go through your Diagnostic report together in a dedicated session. Any programme or Executive Sprint that follows is designed from what the report shows.
