The gap

You know the answer. The room doesn’t always hear it.

A senior stakeholder pushes back and the recommendation starts to soften. A strong idea gets buried under too much detail. A meeting in your second language moves faster than you’d like.

None of this says anything about how capable you are, but it shapes how capable you look. Calidyn works on that gap, for professionals who work across cultures and use English as a working language.

How we work

Start with evidence. Then work on the real thing.

01

The Communication Diagnostic

Every engagement starts here. We look at how you communicate across real work situations, like presenting a recommendation, handling a challenge or writing to someone senior, and find exactly where your message stops landing. You get a clear written report on what’s working and what’s worth developing.

02

The Executive Sprint

From there, we work on your actual emails, meetings and presentations through rewriting, rehearsal and feedback, so the improvement shows up in the work you’re already doing.

The goal is range, not a new personality. Nobody needs to sound more native to be taken seriously.

Who it’s for

For people whose expertise is not in question.

Managers, specialists and leaders in international companies whose communication doesn’t always carry what they know, especially in a second language, under pressure or in front of senior leadership.

If you’ve ever walked out of a meeting knowing you had the best answer in the room, and that it didn’t come across that way, this is for you.
