How I handle your information

Last updated 14 November 2026

Calidyn is run by me, Allegra Bierman. This page explains what information I collect, why, and what you can ask me to do with it. If anything here is unclear, email allegra@calidyn.co.

What I collect

When you send a request through this website, I receive the details you enter: your name, email address, role and company, where you are based, your working language and your description of a work situation.

If you go on to work with me, I will also receive the materials you share for your Diagnostic or Executive Programme, such as recorded responses, writing samples, emails and presentations, along with my own notes and reports about them.

Why I use it

I use your request to reply to you and to decide with you whether a Diagnostic is a good fit. If you become a client, I use your materials only to deliver your Diagnostic, your feedback session and any programme we agree on. I do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

A small number of services help me run Calidyn: FormSubmit delivers the request form to my inbox, Google Workspace hosts my email and files, and a payment provider processes payments. They handle your information only so that these services work.

For client work, I may also use transcription and AI-assisted tools to transcribe and organise your responses. I share only what is needed, and I only use tools whose settings do not allow your material to be used to train their models. The assessment in your report, and every judgement in it, is my own.

Confidentiality

Anything you share about your work, your company or your colleagues stays confidential. I will never publish it or use it as an example, in The Corporate Pulse or anywhere else, unless you have given me clear permission and it has been anonymised.

How long I keep it

If we don’t end up working together, I delete your request within 12 months. If you become a client, I keep your materials for the length of our work and up to 12 months after, unless you ask me to delete them sooner.

Your choices

You can ask me at any time to see the information I hold about you, correct it or delete it. Email allegra@calidyn.co and I will respond within 30 days.

This website

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may record basic technical information such as your IP address.
